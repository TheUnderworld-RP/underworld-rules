Server rules.

Underworld RP is a place to build characters, make connections, and create stories together. There is room for humour, chaos, and imperfect characters, but your actions should make sense in the world. You do not need to be an experienced roleplayer; you do need to give others a fair chance to enjoy their roleplay.

18+

18+ only — no exceptions. All players must be at least 18 years old in real life. Anyone under 18 is not permitted to join or play on Underworld RP. Do not lie about your age or help an underage player bypass this requirement. Underage players will be removed from the server.

01

Put roleplay first.

#Link

Create situations people can take part in. Give others time to speak, react, and make choices. Chasing money, loot, arrests, or kills must not come at the expense of the scene. Losing can move your character's story forward too.

02

Respect the people behind the characters.

#Link

Racism, homophobia, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, real-life threats, doxxing, and out-of-character bullying are prohibited. In-character conflict must stay in character. “It's what my character would do” does not excuse targeting or harassing another player.

03

Stay in character during scenes.

#Link

Keep staff disputes, rule discussions, and real-world arguments out of active roleplay. Avoid comments such as “that's RDM” or “I'm reporting you” in character. Use a clear microphone for voice roleplay and do not disrupt others with soundboards, shouting, or unwanted music.

04

No metagaming.

#Link

Your character can only act on information they learned in character. Do not use streams, Discord calls, private messages, another character's knowledge, or player IDs to gain an advantage. Coordinate active roleplay through in-game communication. Keep your characters' information and assets separate; do not transfer money or items between them through friends.

05

No powergaming or forced outcomes.

#Link

Do not use unrealistic actions or game mechanics to deny someone a fair response. Describe attempted actions rather than deciding another character's reaction: /me attempts to grab their arm gives them room to respond. Do not claim impossible abilities, invent items you do not have, or use animations and menus to escape restraints or consequences.

06

Value your life.

#Link

Treat credible threats as dangerous. If someone has clear control of you at gunpoint and you have no believable way to escape or defend yourself, cooperate. Being threatened does not automatically remove every option, but charging a drawn gun, ignoring a hostage taker, or deliberately getting killed to avoid consequences is not acceptable.

07

No random deathmatch (RDM).

#Link

Do not attack or kill players without a clear in-character reason and appropriate interaction or escalation. Make threats and demands understandable and allow a reasonable chance to respond. Immediate defence against an active attack is allowed. Insults, a minor collision, gang membership, or simply being in an area do not justify killing someone. Saying “hands up” does not automatically justify shooting them a moment later.

08

No vehicle deathmatch (VDM).

#Link

Do not deliberately run people over or repeatedly ram vehicles to injure or kill without a strong roleplay justification. Using a vehicle against someone must be proportionate to an immediate threat. Purposeful contact during a pursuit must fit the situation; repeatedly smashing into people for an easy win is prohibited.

09

Keep driving and injuries believable.

#Link

Drive in a way that suits your vehicle and the situation. Avoid repeated huge jumps, driving up near-vertical terrain, or carrying on normally after a catastrophic crash. Minor bumps do not need a full medical scene, but serious collisions and injuries must affect your roleplay. Being revived does not mean you are instantly fit for another gunfight.

10

Respect downed roleplay and the New Life Rule.

#Link

While downed, do not fight, scout, use your phone or radio, or pass tactical information. Limited injury roleplay is fine if your character is conscious. If revived at the scene, you may remember events but must roleplay your injuries. If you use hospital respawn, forget the events directly leading to your incapacitation and do not rejoin that incident, recover your equipment from it, or seek revenge over it. This does not erase your identity, established relationships, or criminal record. Permanent character death requires the player's agreement.

11

Robberies and kidnappings must create roleplay.

#Link

Player robberies must have an established in-character reason and meaningful roleplay behind them. Robbing players for monetary gain or taking their possessions to sell for profit is prohibited. No “100k or die” demands, regardless of the amount requested. Pointing a weapon at someone and demanding payment does not create a valid roleplay reason, and adding a few lines of dialogue does not make a cash grab acceptable. Any items taken must have a direct roleplay purpose in that situation. Communicate your demands and keep them achievable. Do not arrange fake hostages with friends, repeatedly target the same victim, or rob players simply because they are downed. Do not force bank withdrawals, property transfers, or access to out-of-character accounts. Release hostages once the scene is resolved; do not hold people indefinitely for your entertainment.

12

Give emergency services room to roleplay.

#Link

Police, EMS, and fire personnel are players taking part in the story. Give them opportunities to investigate, negotiate, and provide treatment. Do not repeatedly provoke them just to manufacture chases or shootouts. Targeting an emergency worker needs a specific, credible in-character reason. Emergency workers must also value life and avoid forcing every encounter into an arrest or fight. Abusing job access to supply friends with money or equipment is prohibited; corruption storylines require staff approval.

13

Keep gang conflict proportionate.

#Link

Gang rivalries need an established roleplay basis and meaningful escalation. Gang colours, clothing, or territory alone do not make someone a valid target. Do not drag uninvolved players into disputes, repeatedly target the same people after a scene ends, or use allied groups to bypass any published scene limits. Leave room for negotiation, consequences, and the conflict to end.

14

Do not avoid active roleplay.

#Link

Do not disconnect, respawn, switch characters, store involved vehicles, or use inaccessible interiors to escape an active scene or its consequences. If you crash or disconnect unexpectedly, return as soon as reasonably possible and contact the involved players or staff to resume. If you must leave urgently, let people know where possible. A restart does not automatically cancel an unresolved scene.

15

No cheating, exploits, or economy abuse.

#Link

Cheats, macros that automate gameplay for an advantage, item duplication, bug abuse, and using unauthorised tools to gain information are prohibited. Report exploits privately to staff and stop using them. Do not trade in-game money, items, or services for real money outside official server systems.

16

Protect new players and medical roleplay.

#Link

Do not target players while they are loading in or selecting a character. Do not camp hospitals to rob, abduct, or finish off people receiving treatment. Give newcomers a reasonable opportunity to get started. Players must not deliberately use these protections to escape an active scene; involve staff if the situation cannot be resolved fairly.

17

Respect roleplay boundaries.

#Link

Sexual roleplay and sexual violence are prohibited. Graphic torture or other disturbing scenes require everyone involved to agree out of character beforehand, and must stop if anyone withdraws that agreement. Do not pressure someone to participate or punish them for opting out.

18

Handle reports calmly and follow staff directions.

#Link

For ordinary rule disputes, finish the scene where practical and report it afterwards with the time, player IDs, and available evidence. Do not retaliate or turn the dispute into a public argument. You may disengage from harassment or prohibited content and contact staff immediately. Staff decisions can be appealed respectfully through the designated support channel. Consequences depend on severity, intent, and history; serious abuse or cheating may lead directly to a permanent ban.

These rules apply to everyone and serve as guidelines for fair, enjoyable roleplay. Every situation has its own circumstances, so staff will consider the context, intent and impact on everyone involved when reviewing an incident. Players are expected to follow both the wording and the spirit of the rules. Deliberately working around their wording to ruin someone else's experience can still lead to action. If you are unsure, choose the option that gives everyone a fair chance to roleplay.
